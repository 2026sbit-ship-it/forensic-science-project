# forensic-science-project
A collection of forensic science projects, research, experiments, and educational resources.
This project explores the field of Forensic Science and its role in criminal investigations. It focuses on the scientific examination and analysis of evidence collected from crime scenes, including fingerprints, blood, DNA, hair, fibres, documents, and digital evidence. The project demonstrates how forensic techniques help identify individuals, reconstruct events, and support investigations. It also highlights the importance of accurate evidence collection, preservation, laboratory analysis, and documentation. Through this project, students can understand the practical applications of biology, chemistry, physics, and technology in forensic investigations. The aim is to provide a clear and educational introduction to modern forensic science.
